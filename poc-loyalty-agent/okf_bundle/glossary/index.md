# Business Glossary

This glossary defines standard business terminology, customer segmentation criteria, and loyalty definitions used across the `loyalty_poc` dataset.

## Term Directory
- [Member Spend](/glossary/member-spend.md): Monetary transaction value credited to loyalty members.
- [Visit](/glossary/visit.md): A customer shopping occasion (defined consistently as a distinct member and date occasion or transaction).
- [Purchase](/glossary/purchase.md): A completed transaction resulting in goods exchange.
- [Contactable Member](/glossary/contactable.md): Member with a valid email address and explicit marketing opt-in.
- [Active vs Inactive Member](/glossary/active-inactive-member.md): Activity thresholds (6-month spend, 2-month transaction dormancy).
- [Age Band](/glossary/age-band.md): Standardized demographic age brackets.
- [Loyalty Tier](/glossary/tier.md): Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum ranking and benefit progression.
- [Staff Indicator](/glossary/staff-indicator.md): Indicator distinguishing internal staff (`staff_indicator = 'Y'`).
- [Channel (In-Store vs Online)](/glossary/channel-in-store-vs-online.md): Physical store point-of-sale versus digital e-commerce channels.
- [High Frequency](/glossary/high-frequency.md): Threshold criteria for frequent shopping activity.
